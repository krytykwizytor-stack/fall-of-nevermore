# Historical art sources

The following artworks are historical reproductions, not AI-generated. Both originate from Gustave Doré's illustrations for Edgar Allan Poe's *The Raven* (Harper & Brothers, 1884).

1. **Doré, plate 12.** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dore_The_Raven_1884-12.jpg — public domain; used as cover background.
2. **Doré, plate 22.** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dore_The_Raven_1884-22.jpg — public domain; used in the introductory illustration panel.

Library of Congress edition and rights statement: https://www.loc.gov/item/12037813/ (Children's Book Selections: public domain and free to reuse).

The first import runs through GitHub Actions and saves JPEGs to `public/images/`. The website initially uses Commons image URLs and switches to local images when the files are available.

## Stage III — historical Gothic ornament studies (1831)

Three plates from Augustus Pugin, *Gothic Ornaments, selected from various ancient buildings* (1831), sourced through Wikimedia Commons. Each Commons item is explicitly tagged Public Domain Mark 1.0 (published before 1931; author's life + 70 years elapsed). Images are referenced directly from Commons in this stage; they have not yet been committed as binary files.

- Plate 16: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gothic_Ornaments,_selected_fom_various_ancient_buildings,_both_in_England_and_Franc,_during_the_years_1828,_1829,_and_1839_-_Agustus_Pugin_16.jpeg
- Plate 19: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gothic_Ornaments,_selected_fom_various_ancient_buildings,_both_in_England_and_Franc,_during_the_years_1828,_1829,_and_1839_-_Agustus_Pugin_19.jpeg
- Plate 20: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gothic_Ornaments,_selected_fom_various_ancient_buildings,_both_in_England_and_Franc,_during_the_years_1828,_1829,_and_1839_-_Agustus_Pugin_20.jpeg

No generative AI artwork was used. Ornament glyphs, borders, textures and monograms are implemented with typography and CSS. The FN monogram is decorative and is not an official coat of arms.

## Botanical memorial motifs — stage IV

These are original historical illustrations, not AI-generated imagery. The designs are used as atmospheric references; no particular floral symbol is established as a canonical Ravensmoor family emblem.

- **Ivy, Hedera helix** — Jacob Sturm, *Deutschlands Flora in Abbildungen*, 1796. Public-domain status documented by Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hedera_helix_Sturm12001.jpg . Source image: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Hedera_helix_Sturm12001.jpg
- **Rosa gallica regalis** — Pierre-Joseph Redouté, *Les Roses*, 1824. Public-domain status documented by Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Redoute_-_Rosa_gallica_regalis.jpg . Source image: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Redoute_-_Rosa_gallica_regalis.jpg
